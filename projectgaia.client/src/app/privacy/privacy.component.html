Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Please read this privacy policy carefully.

🔐 1. Introduction

Project Gaia ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our services.

📘 2. Information We Collect

When you register and use Project Gaia, we collect the following personal information:

  • Account Information: Username, email address, and a hashed version of your password
  • Invoice Information: Price, consumption, and date of issue
  • Reminder Information: Name, description, and due date/time of reminders you set

🔄 3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide the following services:

  • Manage and store your account, invoices, and reminders
  • Perform calculations and comparisons (such as in the simulation tab)
  • Send account-related notifications (e.g., email confirmations)
  • Enhance and improve our services based on usage

Your data is never shared with third parties unless explicitly required by law or in cases of service improvement.

⚙️ 4. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. All sensitive information, such as passwords, is securely hashed before being stored.

🧠 5. Data Accuracy

You are responsible for ensuring the accuracy of the data you provide. We rely on the information you input, and it is important that you enter accurate data, especially when using the simulation features of the app.

💡 6. Retention of Data

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. You can request deletion of your data by closing your account.

👥 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we store about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data from our systems (subject to any legal obligations)

If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at project.gaia.bot@gmail.com.

⚖️ 8. Sharing of Data

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except in the following cases:

  • If required by law or to comply with legal processes
  • If necessary to protect our rights, property, or the safety of our users

🛠️ 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices or legal requirements. We will notify users of significant changes. Continued use of Project Gaia constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

📬 10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our practices, please contact us at project.gaia.bot@gmail.com.
